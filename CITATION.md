# Data source and attribution

Vessel traffic data from **IMF PortWatch** (portwatch.imf.org), a joint product of
the International Monetary Fund and the University of Oxford, derived from
satellite AIS signals via the UN Global Platform.

PortWatch figures are **model estimates**, not customs-verified trade records.
Transit counts are more reliable than volume and capacity estimates.

This project is an independent retrospective analysis. It is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or reviewed by the IMF or the University of Oxford.
